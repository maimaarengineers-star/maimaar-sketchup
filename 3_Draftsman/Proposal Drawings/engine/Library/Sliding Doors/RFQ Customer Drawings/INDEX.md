# DOOR DRAWINGS THE CUSTOMER GAVE US — from the RFQ

> *"RFQ = All Customer Data — Request for Quotation."*
> *"Even in 266-26, the customer has also given us a drawing showing the Location and
> details of the Sliding Door in the RFQ."*
> — Nasir, 4-Sep-2026

**380 door sheets from 260 enquiries.**

This is the other half of `Job Door Drawings/`. An **approval** sheet says what WE
drew; an **RFQ** sheet says what the CUSTOMER asked for — and the customer's drawing is
where a door's LOCATION comes from. MSPL-26-266 is the case in point: its RFQ layout
plan puts one 12′ × 12′ door on a side wall and one on the end wall, which is not what
the BSF said until it was corrected against this drawing.

Mined the same way as the approval sets: every PDF in `inquiry_documents`, read page by
page, kept only where a door is the page's SUBJECT and the page reads as a drawing
rather than a BOQ or an invoice. **3,484 RFQ PDFs on disk were read.**

Filed by **customer and proposal number**, so the question *"what did this customer ask
for last time?"* has one place to look.
