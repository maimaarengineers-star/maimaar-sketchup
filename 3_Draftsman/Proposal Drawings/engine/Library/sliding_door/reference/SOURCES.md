## 2 · The reference PEB technical manual

`D:\Design Manual\` (the manual PDF, 932 pages). It is a **design** manual — loads, frames,
purlins and girts — and has no sliding-door detail chapter. What it does have is the **jamb and
door-leaf design**, which is where the member sizes come from:

| File | Pages | What it settles |
|---|---|---|
| `RefManual_p750_C-sections-120mm-are-sliding-door-framing.pdf` | 750 | *"120 mm deep sections are used as framing members of sliding doors."* Framed-opening jambs and headers are C-sections, single or double back-to-back; 200C15 up to 3000 long, 250C when the girt is 250 deep. Connection bolts 12 mm dia HSB Gr. 8.8. C-section rolling limits on the TMCP machine. |
| `RefManual_p753-759_jamb-and-SLIDING-DOOR-leaf-design.pdf` | 753–759 | **Design of Jambs**, with a worked sliding door. Jamb tributary width for a sliding door (or open access) **0.5 m**, against (FO/2 + 0.5) for a roll-up — the reason a sliding door's jamb is so much lighter. Then the DSD leaf itself, 3 m × 6 m in a 6000 × 6000 opening: inner stile span 1.50 / width 1.50 → 120C20; central stile span 6.00 / width 1.50 → 2 × 120C20; edge stile half the central load; bottom stile span 3.00 / width 0.75. **That is a 1500 × 1500 stile-and-rail grid**, and it is the module the drawer uses. |
| `RefManual_framed-opening-jamb-header-pages.pdf` | 594, 636, 713, 717 | Framed openings elsewhere in the manual, for context. |
| `RefManual_p750.png`, `p754`–`p758.png` | — | The same pages as images, for quick eyeballing without opening a PDF. |

## 3 · What the search actually covered

- `E:\Maimaar Steel Pvt Ltd\Jobs` — **29,287 PDFs and 2,297 DWGs**, 2016 → 2026.
  - every DWG grepped for the string `sliding door`
  - every PDF of 40 pages or fewer read page by page, then filtered to the pages that are a
    **drawing** (they say ELEVATION / SECTION / DETAIL / ERECTION DRAWING) rather than a spec form
    that merely lists a sliding door among its accessories
- `D:\Design Manual` — all 932 manual pages searched for `sliding`, `stile`, `DSD`, `jamb`,
  `header`, `framed opening`, `roll-up`.

**Nothing on E: is named "sliding" except three folders**, and that is why the content scan
mattered: it found 444 pages naming a sliding door, of which 75 are actually drawings — including
every one of the 2024/25 sheets above, which a filename search misses completely.

The first sliding door in the archive is **MSPL-027, November 2021**; MSPL-030 follows five months
later (both Awan Sports). The 2019 and 2020 jobs have `DOOR_HEADER & JAMB` sheets but no sliding
door. **The convention then changed**: 2024/25 doors are tube-framed, bottom-rolling on a Ø12 rail,
carry a wicket door, and are dimensioned in feet.
